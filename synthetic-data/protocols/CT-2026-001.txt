1 PROTOCOL SYNOPSIS
Objective: To evaluate the change from baseline in HbA1c at Week 24 with TGX-417 compared with placebo when added to stable metformin therapy.
Population: Adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus inadequately controlled on metformin monotherapy.
Planned enrolment: 240 participants randomised 1:1:1 to TGX-417 low dose, TGX-417 high dose, or placebo.
Duration: 4-week screening period, 24-week treatment period, 4-week safety follow-up.

2 OBJECTIVES AND ENDPOINTS
Primary endpoint: Change from baseline in HbA1c at Week 24.
Secondary endpoints: Change in fasting plasma glucose; proportion of participants achieving HbA1c below 7.0%; change in body weight.
Safety endpoints: Incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events, hypoglycaemia, and clinically relevant laboratory abnormalities.

3 STUDY DESIGN
Multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study conducted at approximately 40 fictional sites.

5 STUDY POPULATION
5.1 Inclusion Criteria
Participants are eligible to be included in the study only if ALL of the following criteria apply:
INC-001. Participant is aged 18 to 75 years, inclusive, at the time of screening.
INC-002. Documented diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus (ICD-10 E11) for at least 180 days prior to screening.
INC-003. Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) between 7.0% and 10.5%, inclusive, measured within 30 days prior to screening.
INC-004. Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) greater than or equal to 60 mL/min/1.73m2, measured within 30 days prior to screening.
INC-005. Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) less than or equal to 100 U/L, measured within 30 days prior to screening.
INC-006. Aspartate aminotransferase (AST) less than or equal to 100 U/L, measured within 30 days prior to screening.
INC-007. Total bilirubin less than or equal to 1.8 mg/dL, measured within 30 days prior to screening.
INC-008. Receiving metformin at a stable dose for at least 90 days prior to screening.
INC-009. Body mass index (BMI) between 25.0 and 40.0 kg/m2, inclusive, measured within 30 days prior to screening.
INC-010. Written informed consent obtained prior to any study-specific procedure.

5.2 Exclusion Criteria
Participants are excluded from the study if ANY of the following criteria apply:
EXC-001. Diagnosis of type 1 diabetes mellitus or any history of diabetic ketoacidosis.
EXC-002. Myocardial infarction, stroke, or hospitalization for heart failure within 180 days prior to screening.
EXC-003. Major surgery or percutaneous coronary intervention within 90 days prior to screening.
EXC-004. Positive pregnancy test at screening (female participants only).
EXC-005. Use of systemic corticosteroids within 30 days prior to screening.
EXC-006. Use of any GLP-1 receptor agonist or insulin within 90 days prior to screening.
EXC-007. Any history of acute or chronic pancreatitis.
EXC-008. Diagnosis of malignancy within 5 years prior to screening.

5.3 Investigator Guidance (Non-Binding)
GDN-001. Participants should have adequate renal function in the opinion of the investigator.

6 STUDY TREATMENT
TGX-417 or matching placebo is administered orally once daily with the first meal of the day. Participants continue their stable metformin dose throughout the study.

7 SAFETY MONITORING
Hypoglycaemia events, liver enzyme elevations above 3 x ULN, and renal function decline must be reported to the medical monitor within 24 hours.
